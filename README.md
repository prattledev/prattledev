# Hi, I'm Matt Thomas

Broadcast and AoIP engineer with 15+ years of experience designing and delivering mission-critical audio and communications systems for live events. My work spans major football tournaments, global elections, and the Olympic Games - environments where failure isn't an option.

I operate at the intersection of broadcast and IT, and I hold a Cisco CCNA certification (2020). I write about the systems that keep live events on air at [matt-thomas.work](https://matt-thomas.work).

---

## Projects

**[RavennaCalc](https://www.ravennacalc.com)** - A browser-based bandwidth calculator for Ravenna/AES67 audio streams. Built to replace a clunky spreadsheet used when planning temporary WAN services for major sports events, where fibre is expensive and over-provisioning costs money. ([source](https://github.com/prattledev/ravennacalc.com))

**[Comrex Fleet Dashboard](https://github.com/prattledev/comrex-dashboard)** - A lightweight Node.js dashboard for monitoring a fleet of Comrex broadcast audio codecs. Polls the Comrex Switchboard API and presents registration status, connection state, firmware version, and IP address for every unit in a single auto-refreshing view.

**[GLITS & BLITS Generator](https://github.com/prattledev/glits-and-blits-gen)** - A web-based generator for broadcast-standard test tone sequences. Produces GLITS (the BBC/EBU stereo alignment cycle) and BLITS (the EBU 5.1 surround channel identification sequence) as 24-bit PCM WAV files at 48 kHz, with correct WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE headers. Python backend, vanilla JS frontend, deployable via Docker.
